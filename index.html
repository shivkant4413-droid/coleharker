About the book

Kade Vance is a Senior QA Analyst. In practice, that means he gets paid to notice everything wrong with a game that everyone else gets to pretend is finished. One hundred and eighty-three bug reports since March. All of them tagged Won't Fix.

At 3:12 a.m., in the middle of crunch, his heart gives out.

He wakes up in a rain-soaked alley in the guild city of Ravenhold, younger, in a body that isn't quite his, with a health bar floating in the corner of his vision. The world's System greets him as an unregistered soul. Then, when he tries to swipe the message away out of habit, it opens something nobody was ever meant to find.

In Ravenhold, an Altar assigns your Class at fifteen, and your Class decides the rest of your life. Kade's comes back as Debugger, a Class that isn't on any list. He's ranked F, the bookmakers put him at forty-to-one against the city's golden boy, and the System is starting to notice that someone is poking at its seams.

Every system has an exploit. Kade has always been the one who finds it first.

Patch Notes is the first book in The Exploiter, a progression fantasy series for readers who'd rather find the exploit than grind the level. It's for fans of isekai light novels, system apocalypse, and underdog-breaks-the-rules GameLit.
